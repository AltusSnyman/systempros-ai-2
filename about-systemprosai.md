## The Product Suite

### SP-01 — The Revenue Triad (Voice AI)
Three AI agents working in concert to close the gap between a business's phone line and its revenue.

- **Receptionist agent**: Answers every inbound call 24/7, sounds human, qualifies callers, books directly into the CRM or calendar.
- **Speed-to-lead agent**: When a web form or ad lead comes in, this agent calls back within 10 seconds — before the lead cools or calls a competitor.
- **Reactivation agent**: Works the dormant contacts in the CRM automatically, re-engaging old leads and past clients without manual effort.

Built on Telnyx, Twilio, or Vonage. Integrates with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, ServiceTitan, Jobber, and most major CRMs.

### SP-02 — Lead Gen Websites
A client's existing website is re-engineered into a 24/7 capture machine. Smart qualifying forms, embedded booking calendars, live chat widgets, voice widgets, and automation triggers — all wired together so every visitor becomes a lead and every lead becomes a contact in the pipeline.

### SP-03 — The Lead Reactor
Built for businesses running Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads. When a lead comes in through an ad, the Reactor launches a multi-channel outreach sequence — iMessage, WhatsApp, SMS, and voice — simultaneously, within seconds. It pursues the lead across channels until they book or opt out. Average first response: under 10 seconds.

### SP-04 — The Private Assistant (OpenClaw)
A done-for-you AI assistant based on the open-source OpenClaw platform (247,000+ GitHub stars). Installed on WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, or any messaging platform the client uses. It has persistent memory, a custom personality, access to the client's data and tools, and can be extended with 3,000+ community-built skills. Unlike ChatGPT, it runs on the client's own infrastructure and never shares data externally.

### SP-05 — Consulting
A two-part engagement:
1. **Blueprint**: A forensic audit of the client's current operations — phone coverage, lead response time, workflow bottlenecks, tool stack, compliance exposure. Delivered as a strategic AI roadmap with ROI projections, implementation timelines, and a security review.
2. **Turnkey**: Full-service implementation. SystemPros designs and builds the systems identified in the Blueprint phase, then hands them over commissioned and running.

Regional specialists for Australia and New Zealand, covering local compliance (privacy laws, GST, employment frameworks).

### SP-06 — Training
1-on-1 hands-on sessions for business owners and their teams. Custom curriculum built around what the client actually uses. Core topics: Claude Code, GoHighLevel, N8N, Make.com, Voice AI configuration, OpenClaw/private assistant setup. Sessions are recorded for the team. No generic courses — every session is built around the client's specific tools and goals.

## How We Work

### The Commissioning Standard

We use the word "commission" deliberately. In engineering, a system is commissioned when it is installed, tested, verified, and handed over as a working unit — not when it is merely configured. Every SystemPros installation goes through the same standard: scoped, built, tested against real calls and real leads, then handed over with documentation and a live handoff.

We do not sell subscriptions to tools. We do not hand clients a login and a tutorial. We build and commission, then support what we've built.

### Typical Engagement Flow

1. **Free audit call (30–60 minutes)**: We map the client's current call answer rate, lead response time, tool stack, and the dollar value of what they're losing. We produce a concrete number — not an estimate, a measurable gap.
2. **Scoping and proposal**: We design the specific system that closes that gap. Fixed price, fixed scope. No surprises.
3. **Build and integration**: We connect to the client's existing CRM, calendar, and phone number. They do not need to change tools. Most deployments go live within 48 hours.
4. **Commissioning**: We run live call tests and lead injection tests to verify everything works before we call it done.
5. **Handover and support**: We walk the client (and relevant staff) through what was built. Ongoing support available.

### Technical Philosophy

- **We integrate, we don't replace.** The client's existing CRM, calendar, and job management platform stay in place. We wire AI around them, not over them.
- **We build on proven infrastructure.** Voice AI runs on Telnyx, Twilio, or Vonage — carriers with enterprise-grade uptime. AI brains run on the best available models (GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini) depending on the use case.
- **We do not build fragile automations.** N8N and Make.com pipelines are built with error handling, fallback paths, and monitoring. If a step fails, a human is notified — not left in the dark.
- **We take security seriously.** Private AI installations run on the client's own infrastructure. Data does not leave their environment. Compliance with NZ Privacy Act, Australian Privacy Act, and US requirements is reviewed on every engagement.
- **We measure in revenue, not impressions.** Every system we build has a measurable output: calls answered, jobs booked, leads responded to, contracts signed.

### Pricing Philosophy

We price on value delivered, not on hours spent. A system that adds $10,000/month in captured revenue is not priced at $500 because it took one day to build. Retainer pricing is available for ongoing management and optimisation.
