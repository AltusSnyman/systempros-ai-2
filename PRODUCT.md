# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and managers of revenue-hungry local businesses — roofers, dentists, chiropractors, landscapers, auto detailers, therapists, coaches, senior-living operators — in New Zealand, Australia, and the USA. They are not technologists. They open this site at the shop counter or between patients, on a phone or an aging laptop, in daylight. Their job-to-be-done: stop losing leads (missed calls, slow follow-up, dead databases) and figure out whether these "AI people" are trustworthy operators or hype merchants. Secondary audience: SMB operators curious about a private AI assistant (OpenClaw) and hands-on AI training.

## Product Purpose

SystemPros.ai sells six installed AI systems, not software licenses:

1. **The Revenue Triad** (`/voice-ai`) — three voice AI agents: 24/7 inbound receptionist, instant outbound calls on new web leads, database reactivation.
2. **Lead Gen Websites** (`/lead-gen-websites`) — existing sites re-engineered into lead capture machines (smart forms, calendars, chat/voice widgets, automations).
3. **The Lead Reactor** (`/lead-reactor`) — Meta ads to booked appointments via <10s multi-channel outreach (iMessage, WhatsApp, SMS, calls).
4. **The Private Assistant** (`/private-assistant`) — done-for-you OpenClaw AI assistant on WhatsApp/Telegram/Slack, 3,000+ skills, persistent memory.
5. **Consultation** (`/consulting`) — forensic operations audit + strategic AI roadmap, backed by 30 years of enterprise IT architecture.
6. **Training** (`/training`) — 1-on-1 sessions on Claude Code, GoHighLevel, N8N, Make.com, Voice AI, OpenClaw.

Success = a visitor believes "these are real engineers who install real systems," and books a free consultation. The single conversion action across the site is **Book a Free Consultation** (`/contact`).

## Brand Personality

**Seasoned. Engineered. Plainspoken.**

The voice of a 30-year systems engineer who has commissioned infrastructure that cannot fail — now applying that discipline to a dentist's phone line. Confident without hype. Specific numbers over adjectives. The site should feel like a signed-off installation dossier: measured, precise, quietly proud of its work. Emotional goal: *relief* ("finally, adults in the room") and *certainty* ("this will work because it's engineered, not vibed").

## Anti-references

- **The old SystemPros site and every 2024–2026 AI-agency template**: near-black background, electric-blue glow, glassmorphism cards, gradient text, purple/blue "AI" palette, hero-metric stat bars.
- **Generic SaaS landing kits**: identical icon-card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrow labels over every section, numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding everywhere.
- **Editorial-magazine affectation**: italic display serifs, drop caps, mono-label + ruled-column Klim pastiche. We are engineers, not a type foundry.
- **Cream/beige "warm minimal" AI default.**

## Design Principles

1. **Dossier, not dashboard.** The site reads like a commissioning document for infrastructure: schematics, spec tables, tolerances, sign-off stamps. Show engineering, don't claim it.
2. **Daylight confidence.** Light surfaces, deep committed green ink. Trust is built in daylight; hype lives in dark mode.
3. **Numbers carry the sale.** "Under 10 seconds", "24/7", "30 years", "3 countries" — concrete figures set in the data face, never vague superlatives.
4. **Motion is machinery.** Animations behave like systems coming online — lines drawing, switches engaging, precise easing — never floaty decoration. Every animation has a reduced-motion fallback.
5. **One action.** Every page funnels to Book a Free Consultation. No competing CTAs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 (verify — light-gray-on-white is the enemy). Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation. Semantic landmarks, skip link, keyboard-reachable nav/dropdowns, focus-visible states in brand green. Audience skews 40–65; default body size ≥16px, generous tap targets.
