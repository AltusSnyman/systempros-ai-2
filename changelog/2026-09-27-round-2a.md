## Why
Search Console URL inspection of all 51 sitemap URLs (2026-09-27): 39 indexed, 6 crawled-not-indexed, 3 discovered, 3 unknown. /contact/ was crawled but not indexed (last crawl 2026-09-13): little text beyond a booking iframe. Thin landscaping/"AI business solutions" pages dilute the crawl budget of a 51-URL site. DataForSEO AU: "ai receptionist" 880/mo, "ai receptionist australia" 390, "ai answering service" 260, "ai receptionist cost" 20; the People Also Ask question "How much does an AI receptionist cost?" shows on every related query and Google's AI Overview quotes $14–$600/month, so a sourced price page can earn the PAA/AI Overview citation for the whole cluster. The home video (90 MB, autoplay on load) and the mobile sideways scroll were round-1 leftovers.
